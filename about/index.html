Why dogs · A note from Wubbushi

The drawings that would
have been on the fridge.

Nobody has a picture.

My other collection, HOLY, is an evangelical adolescence between 1975 and 1995: church buses, revival carpet, motel pools, baptisms, hard flash. My mom's photo albums were what she prized most. They were lost in hasty moves and storage units nobody kept paying for, and by the time I was ready to go back for them, and I mean ready in the way you have to be ready for something like that, there was nothing left to restore. Nobody has a picture of those years. Nobody photographs the part you remember anyway.

A dog for you.

I want to make little paintings for the people who become part of my life.

ALL DOGS is a journal for me. Each dog is a chance to remember a person and the time when we got to know each other. I hope I'm still adding to it twenty years from now.

I built a paintbrush in code to make these dogs. The paint runs out as it moves, and the surface shows through. I want them to feel like the drawings someone made for you as a kid, the kind you put on the fridge.

When a dog goes home with you, your name goes on his collar. You give him a name too. That first entry stays with him as his story continues.

I hope you'll keep him. Years from now, I'd like to look through this collection and remember a friend with each dog.

The medium

The paint is code.

I've been building a brush in code. Each bristle carries a finite amount of paint, picks up what's still wet and leaves a mark where it touches the surface. Long strokes run dry. I write each dog's composition into the painter: its face, clothes, pose and the world around it.

Watch her take shape.

The ground is ready. Now the dog: her gown, her wig, her face, the little cake. Watch the paint travel across the canvas as the original brushwork unfolds.

Look at what the brush leaves.

Drag white through wet grey and the brush picks up grey. As it moves, the clean colour comes back. A long stroke thins into streaks. The weave shows through where the paint is thin.

Paint with the same brush.

The paint kit contains the brush behind these dogs: bristles, wet colour mixing, dry strokes and the canvas underneath. Use it to make something of your own.
