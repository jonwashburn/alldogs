Don't know an owner yet?

The first owners are invited by Jonathan, starting with his collectors. Ask the person who sent you here to introduce you. The wider pack grows through existing owners.

How will you know it is really me?

We contact the exact X account in your application. Reply from that account with your application receipt. We separately check your invitation with the person who vouched for you. A typed handle alone is never verification.

Will I put down a deposit?

No money is collected for this waitlist. Payment opens only with a confirmed invitation, a stated price and clear terms. Never send money or a seed phrase in a DM.
