How to adopt

  1. 01

    Apply and share your link.

    Enter your X handle. Add your Ethereum address if you have one, or ask us to help with a wallet. Share your application link to find an owner to vouch for you. You can apply before you know anyone.

  2. 02

    An owner vouches for you.

    An eligible owner opens your link and signs in with X to vouch. No wallet connection. Their vouch lasts five days. Wubbushi reviews applications and invites the founding owners himself.

  3. 03

    Choose. Name. Bring home.

    Once approved, you'll hear from Wubbushi when your turn is ready. Log into the private gallery, choose your dog and give it a name. Confirm your adoption to mint the artwork directly to your wallet. There is nothing to pay upfront.

    You then have seven days from receiving your dog to pay Wubbushi what you think it is worth. The amount you pay is recorded as your valuation in the dog's attributes. After seven days, that record is permanent.

Wubbushi keeps adding paintings, so the collection has no fixed final size. Each person still gets only one direct adoption, ever.

An application or a vouch does not guarantee acceptance or reserve a dog.

Don't know an owner yet?

Apply anyway. Share your application link on X and introduce yourself. An eligible owner can vouch through that page. Wubbushi invites the founding owners, so the register may be empty while applications are open.

Who is eligible to vouch?

You can vouch if you own a dog, have never sold it, and none of the people you directly vouched for has ever sold their dog. If one of those people vouches for someone who later sells, that sale does not count against you.

Infection and natural death do not count as sales. A sale that creates an immortal still counts.

There is no lifetime limit to vouches, but only three can be pending at once. A new vouch expires after five days. If Wubbushi invites the person within that time, the slot stays in use until they adopt or the invitation is withdrawn.

How will you know it is really me?

Wubbushi will contact the exact X account you submit and ask for your private receipt. Keep it off your public application page and out of your posts. Owners sign in with X to vouch, without connecting a wallet. Before adoption, we’ll confirm the receiving address with you or help you set up a wallet.

Will I put down a deposit?

No deposit, and no payment before adoption. Once approved, you choose and name your dog in the private gallery, then confirm the adoption to mint it directly to your wallet. You have seven days from receiving it to pay Wubbushi what you think it is worth. That amount becomes a permanent attribute at the end of the seven days. Never share a private key or recovery phrase.
