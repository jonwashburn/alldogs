Don't know an owner yet?

That is what your application link is for. Share it on X and introduce yourself. Any eligible dog owner can open it and vouch for you. Wubbushi invites the founding owners.

Who is eligible to vouch?

An owner who has never sold their dog, and whose direct invitees have never sold theirs. Unlimited people over time, but only three outstanding vouches at once. An adoption or a withdrawn vouch frees a slot. Natural death and infection are not sales. A sale by an invitee's invitee does not count against you.

How will you know it is really me?

We contact the exact X account in your application and ask for your private receipt. A typed handle or address is not verification. Owners prove their vouch with a wallet signature; buyers will prove control of their submitted wallet at checkout.

Will I put down a deposit?

No money is collected for this waitlist. Payment opens only with a confirmed invitation, a stated price and clear terms. Never send money or a seed phrase in a DM.
