Meet me on X ↗

Get to know the person before vouching. Wubbushi reviews applications and checks identities. The wallet address and private receipt are never shown here.

Connect the Ethereum wallet registered with your confirmed adoption, then sign the message to vouch. Use a wallet browser or browser extension that supports standard message signing. Smart-contract wallets are not supported yet. Vouching requires no transaction, payment or token approval. Never give anyone your private key or recovery phrase.

What does my vouch mean?

You are recommending this person to Wubbushi. Your vouch stays pending until they adopt or you withdraw it, using one of your three slots. There is no lifetime limit to how many people you can vouch for.

You can vouch if you have never sold your dog and none of the people you directly vouched for has ever sold their dog. If one of those people vouches for someone who later sells, that sale does not count against you.

Infection and natural death do not count as sales. A sale that creates an immortal still counts.

Wubbushi reviews the application and checks the applicant's identity. If accepted, they pay through the official adoption flow, then choose and name their dog in a private viewing. A vouch does not guarantee acceptance. Paid adoption and private selection are not open yet.
