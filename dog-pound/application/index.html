An application, not a verified identity or a reserved dog. Get to know the person before you vouch.

Use the wallet registered with your confirmed adoption. This first version supports injected Ethereum wallets with standard message signing, not smart-contract wallets. We never request payment, a token approval, a private key or a recovery phrase to vouch.

What does my vouch mean?

One person you believe belongs in the pack. It uses one of your three outstanding slots until they adopt or you withdraw. There is no lifetime limit to your vouches. If you sell your dog, or any of your direct invitees sells theirs, you cannot vouch again. A vouch is not payment or a guarantee of adoption.
