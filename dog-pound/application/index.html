What does my vouch mean?

You are recommending this person to Wubbushi. Your vouch lasts five days and uses one of your three slots. If Wubbushi invites the person within those five days, the slot stays in use until they adopt or the invitation is withdrawn. There is no lifetime limit to how many people you can vouch for.

You can vouch if you have never sold your dog and none of the people you directly vouched for has ever sold their dog. If one of those people vouches for someone who later sells, that sale does not count against you.

Infection and natural death do not count as sales. A sale that creates an immortal still counts.

Wubbushi reviews the application and checks the applicant's identity. Once approved, they receive an invitation to log into the private gallery, choose a dog and name it. Confirming the adoption mints the artwork directly to their wallet, with nothing to pay upfront.

They have seven days from receiving their dog to pay Wubbushi what they think it is worth. The amount paid is recorded as their valuation in the dog's attributes and becomes permanent after seven days.

A vouch does not guarantee acceptance. Applications are open. X sign-in is being connected; minting and artist payments are not open yet.
