Looking for someone to vouch for you? Here you can see who has adopted a dog and who is eligible to vouch.

Who can vouch?

You can vouch if you have never sold your dog and none of the people you directly vouched for has ever sold their dog. If one of those people vouches for someone who later sells, that sale does not count against you.

Infection and natural death do not count as sales. A sale that creates an immortal still counts.

The starting allowance is one vouch per person per week, on a rolling seven-day basis. Your account shows your current allowance. Wubbushi has unlimited vouches. Every vouch is permanent. Wubbushi can disable someone’s ability to issue new vouches.

To vouch, open an applicant's public link and sign in with X. No wallet connection. Wubbushi still reviews the application; your vouch does not guarantee acceptance.
