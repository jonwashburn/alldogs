Digital paintings by Wubbushi

To sell your dog,
you have to kill it.

ALL DOGS is a growing collection of digital paintings. You adopt a dog, give it a name, and live with it through a zombie apocalypse.

Sell a living dog and it dies, coming back as a zombie. Each new zombie has a 50% chance of killing someone else's dog and turning it into a zombie too. The chain can keep going. Your sale and your name stay on every dog it reaches.

On its first resale, a dog has a 1 in 20 chance to resist and become immortal. Holding on cannot prevent infection or natural death. The collection rules.

A note from Wubbushi

The drawings that would
have been on the fridge.

ALL DOGS comes from the same childhood as HOLY, my collection about the photographs we lost. I wrote a program to paint the way a kid paints: a fat outline, paint running out mid-stroke, an eye a little wrong. I can still paint the dogs.

An invitation

A dog of your own.

Apply to enter the Pound and choose up to three favourites. Find an owner to vouch for you. Once approved, meet the three dogs Wubbushi chooses for your private viewing. Choose one. Give it a name.

You receive the artwork first. Then you have seven days to pay Wubbushi the value you choose for the art and your participation in it. Your valuation becomes part of your dog's record.

One direct adoption per person, ever. The collection keeps growing. Your allowance does not reset.

Applying is free and does not reserve a dog.
