One season. Everyone adopts one pet for the fee; the whole gate is the bank. There are exactly threshold × N red seats with prices fixed in advance, first seat highest, last seat almost nothing. Killing your pet takes the next seat, escrows its price as a bet on collapse, and the zombie trades immediately. If every seat fills, red wins instantly: killers are paid their seats, the artist takes 50%, and every living pet is locked on death row, tradable only as a zombie once it dies. If the year ends with seats open, blue wins: every zombie comes back to life, the faithful and the angels are refunded 90% of the fee, every blue dog is locked living and freed to trade, and the artist keeps the rest. A few pets die naturally each week and become angels: out of the game and out of the tipping ratio, tradable immediately, refunded on blue.

The one open rule: how much of the refund does a killer keep when blue wins? Killer refund on blue is the price of grace; slide it up and watch where faith stops being possible. Money math exact; crowd is a toy: greed, herd, need shocks, optional red army raid.
