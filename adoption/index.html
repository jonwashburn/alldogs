How to adopt

How a dog becomes yours.

Join the waitlist, then step into the Dog Pound. Choose up to three favourites and put them in order. Your wishlist helps Wubbushi prepare your private viewing. You can find an owner to vouch for you afterward.

01

Apply. Meet the dogs.

Enter your X handle. Add your Ethereum address if you have one, or ask us to help with a wallet. No payment or wallet connection is needed. Then enter the Pound and make your wishlist. Share your application link to find a vouch; keep your private receipt to yourself.

02

An owner vouches for you.

The owner opens your link and signs in with X to vouch. No wallet connection. A new vouch lasts five days. Wubbushi reviews your application and checks your identity. A vouch does not guarantee acceptance.

03

Choose. Name. Bring home.

Once approved, you'll hear from Wubbushi when your turn is ready. Wubbushi uses your wishlist to curate a private viewing. Log in, meet the three dogs chosen for you, and choose one to name and bring home. Confirm your adoption to mint the artwork directly to your wallet. There is nothing to pay upfront.

The dog is yours. The value is yours to decide. Participation in ALL DOGS, a conceptual artwork by Wubbushi. You receive your dog first, then decide what the experience is worth to you. You have seven days from the successful mint timestamp to pay Wubbushi the amount you choose. Your valuation becomes part of the artwork.

Who can vouch?

You can vouch if you own a dog, have never sold it, and none of the people you directly vouched for has ever sold their dog. If one of those people vouches for someone who later sells, that sale does not count against you.

Infection and natural death do not count as sales. A sale that creates an immortal still counts.

There is no lifetime limit to vouches, but only three can be pending at once. A new vouch expires after five days. If Wubbushi invites the person within that time, the slot stays in use until they adopt or the invitation is withdrawn.

How does the pack begin?

Wubbushi invites the founding owners. After that, eligible owners vouch for new applicants. He keeps painting and adding dogs, so the collection has no fixed final size.

ONE DIRECT ADOPTION. EVER.Each person may adopt one dog directly, ever. Selling it, a change of state or new dogs joining the collection never gives you another direct adoption. This limit does not cover secondary-market purchases.

Applications are open. X sign-in is being connected. Minting, artist payments and gameplay are not open yet. Applying is free and does not reserve a dog. After you apply, the Dog Pound opens for your wishlist.

The collection rules

The zombie apocalypse.

Your dog starts living when you adopt it. The sale rules below apply when an owner sells a dog after adoption. The first adoption itself does not turn a dog into a zombie.

01

Selling your dog means killing it.

Selling a living dog normally kills it. It comes back as a zombie. On its first resale only, the dog has a 1 in 20 chance to resist and become an immortal hero instead.

02

The infection can spread.

Each new zombie has a 50% chance to infect and kill one other living dog, turning that dog into a zombie too. The newly infected dog gets the same chance to infect one more. The chain can stop after any dog, or keep going.

If your sale starts the chain, your X handle and the sale are permanently recorded on your dog and every dog infected by that chain. The first infected dog is generation one, the next is generation two, and so on. Every generation traces back to the original sale.

03

An immortal can rescue a dog.

Each immortal returns one infection-created zombie to living. A dog turned into a zombie by a sale cannot be rescued. Immortals also get a new background and one of five powers.

All dogs go to heaven

Living dogs
can become angels.

Every eligible living dog has a 1 in 1,600 chance of natural death each day (0.0625%). The dog becomes an angel, and you keep the artwork. Its lifespan and cause of death stay in the record.

Natural death does not spread infection. Holding on to your dog prevents a sale; it cannot guarantee a long life.

The collar

A name tag
with a memory.

The collar records who adopted the dog and what happened to it. That history stays with the dog through every change: living, zombie, immortal or angel.
