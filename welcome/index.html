ALL DOGS

A zombie apocalypse.
With dogs you love.

Look closely. ↓

He thinks he's your dog.

To sell your living dog, you have to kill it.

Sell a living dog and it dies, coming back as a zombie. Each new zombie has a 50% chance of killing someone else's dog and turning it into a zombie too. That dog gets the same chance. And so on.

You get the money. Another collector can lose the dog they loved. Your sale and your name stay on every dog it reaches.

paint the dogs.

HOLY is about the photographs we lost, the missing record of my childhood. ALL DOGS is about the childhood I can’t get back.

I wrote a program to paint the way a kid paints: a fat outline, paint running out mid-stroke, an eye a little wrong. These are the drawings that would have been on the fridge.
